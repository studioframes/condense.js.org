The Condense Blog is where we share the stories behind the project—not every commit, but the updates that matter.

While our **Changelog** documents every release in detail, this blog focuses on engineering insights, major milestones, security announcements, and the ideas that shape Condense.

## What You'll Find Here

### Engineering

Deep dives into the architecture of Condense, performance optimizations, in-memory processing, streaming pipelines, and the design decisions behind the project.

### Release Highlights

Articles covering **major** and **minor** releases, introducing significant new features, improvements, and changes. Routine patch releases remain documented in the Changelog.

### Security

Important security advisories, vulnerability disclosures, mitigation guidance, and recommendations for keeping your applications up to date.

### Tutorials & Guides

Practical walkthroughs, integration examples, and best practices for getting the most out of Condense.

## Why a Blog?

Some topics deserve more than release notes.

A new architecture, a breakthrough optimization, or an important security update often benefits from additional context. The blog provides a place to explain the reasoning behind changes, explore technical concepts in depth, and share knowledge with the community.

## Stay Tuned

Whether you're interested in high-performance Node.js tooling, software optimization, or the evolution of Condense itself, this is where we'll share the stories behind the code.
